Design Protection & Registration

Safeguard the unique appearance of your products and packaging.

What Are Design Rights?

Design rights protect the appearance of a product or its packaging. Unlike patents, which protect how a product works, or trade marks, which protect brand names and logos, design rights cover the visual aspects of a product【63636993120536†L123-L139】. Aspects that can be protected include lines, contours, colours, shape, texture, materials and ornamentation【63636993120536†L123-L139】.

Requirements for Registration

To obtain a registered design, two main conditions must be met【63636993120536†L140-L149】:

  • The design must be new; there is often a 12‑month grace period during which a product can be tested in the market, but early filing is recommended to avoid losing novelty【63636993120536†L144-L147】.
  • The design must have distinctive individual character compared with existing designs【63636993120536†L148-L149】.

Design rights do not cover the function of the product or parts that are not visible during normal use【63636993120536†L151-L152】.

Registered vs. Unregistered

Unregistered design rights arise automatically in jurisdictions where the design is first disclosed【63636993120536†L154-L160】. In the European Union, such protection lasts three years; in the UK it lasts up to ten years【63636993120536†L159-L162】. However, enforcing an unregistered design right requires proving that you created the design and that it was copied【63636993120536†L164-L167】.

Registered design rights offer stronger protection. Registration is quick and inexpensive, provides full protection for up to 25 years and makes enforcement easier for customs officers and courts【63636993120536†L170-L179】.
