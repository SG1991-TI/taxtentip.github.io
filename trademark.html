Trademark Registration & Protection

Build a brand that stands out and learn why registering your mark matters.

What is a Trademark?

A trademark is a word, phrase, symbol, design or a combination of these elements that identifies and distinguishes the source of one party’s goods or services from those of others【916398317048560†L62-L69】. Audible signs, colour schemes and even scent marks can sometimes function as trade marks, but domain names do not qualify【916398317048560†L62-L74】. A service mark performs the same function for services rather than goods【916398317048560†L62-L69】.

Why Register?

Registering a trademark is not mandatory—owners acquire common‑law rights by using a mark in commerce—but unregistered rights are geographically limited and harder to enforce. Federal registration offers significant advantages:

  • Exclusive rights: Registration grants owners the exclusive right to use the mark nationwide in connection with their goods or services, enabling licensing for royalties【916398317048560†L84-L94】.
  • Legal presumptions: A registered mark enjoys a presumption of ownership and validity, allowing owners to use the ® symbol and pursue federal court actions【916398317048560†L115-L130】.
  • Public notice: Registration publicly records your claim to the mark and helps deter infringers【916398317048560†L115-L130】.
  • Customs enforcement: A registered mark can be recorded with customs authorities to block importation of infringing goods【916398317048560†L115-L130】.
  • International benefits: A federal registration makes it easier to obtain protection in foreign jurisdictions and serves as the basis for filings under the Madrid Protocol.
